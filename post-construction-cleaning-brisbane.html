Post Construction Clean questions

When should I book a post-construction clean?

As soon as you have a handover date. We can plan the three phases around your trades so the final clean happens right before the keys change hands.

Why three phases?

Construction dust keeps settling for days after the work stops. Cleaning in stages removes the heavy debris first, then the detail, then any dust that resettles, so the finish at handover actually lasts.

Do you work with builders on an ongoing basis?

Yes. Several local builders use us for every handover. We fit around your schedule and we take pride in making your work look its best.

Which areas do you cover for builders cleans?

Brisbane Bayside and south-east Brisbane. We've completed builders cleans in suburbs including Camp Hill, Coorparoo and West End, as well as across the Redlands.
