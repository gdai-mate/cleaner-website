A deep clean is the reset button for a home. We get into the corners, edges and build-up that a weekly wipe-over leaves behind, so the whole place feels new again when you walk in.

We're a small boutique team based in Birkdale. We give every deep clean the time it actually needs, which is why our clients book us when the job has to be done properly the first time.

When people book a deep clean

Before or after a big event

Hosting Christmas, a birthday or family staying over. Get the house guest-ready without losing a weekend to it.

Selling, moving or settling in

Presenting a home for sale, getting a new place ready to move into, or freshening up after the removalists leave.

Spring or seasonal reset

Brisbane humidity builds up grime and mould fast. A seasonal deep clean knocks it back before it takes hold.

Starting a regular service

Most recurring clients start with a deep clean so every visit after that is maintaining a high standard.

What a deep clean usually covers

  • Skirting boards, door frames, handles and light switches
  • Kitchen benches, cupboard fronts, splashback, stovetop and rangehood
  • Inside the microwave, with oven and fridge interiors on request
  • Showers, screens, tiles, grout lines, taps and fixtures polished
  • Toilets cleaned and sanitised, inside and out
  • Window sills, tracks and reachable glass
  • Ceiling fans, light fittings and air-con vent covers dusted
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, including under movable furniture

Every deep clean is customised. We'll walk through your priorities when we quote and agree the list with you before we start.

One-off Deep Clean questions

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home. A deep clean takes noticeably longer than a regular clean because we work through every room in detail. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote so you know what to expect.

How much does a deep clean cost in Brisbane?

Every home is different, so we quote each job individually rather than using a flat rate. Call 0433 147 018 or request a quote online and we'll give you a clear price before any work starts.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Plenty of our clients leave us a key or access code. We handle your home, privacy and valuables with complete discretion.

What's the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular clean maintains a home that's already in good shape. A deep clean tackles built-up grime in the places that get skipped day to day, like grout, skirting boards, tracks, fans and fittings.

Which areas do you cover for deep cleans?

We're based in Birkdale and cover Brisbane Bayside, including Wellington Point, Cleveland, Thornlands, Redland Bay and Victoria Point, as well as suburbs across south-east Brisbane such as Norman Park, Camp Hill and Coorparoo.
