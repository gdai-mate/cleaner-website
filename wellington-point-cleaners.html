Cleaning near the water

Living this close to the bay is the dream, but salt air leaves a film on glass, sliding doors and outdoor furniture, and sand from the reserve and the King Island walk finds its way into tracks and floors.

Waterfront and near-water homes also cop extra humidity, which means mould in bathrooms and wardrobes over summer. We pay attention to glass, door tracks and wet areas so your home stays fresh, not just tidy.
