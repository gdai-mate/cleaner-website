When someone is recovering from a serious injury, a clean and healthy home matters more than ever. We're an approved provider for NIISQ (the National Injury Insurance Scheme, Queensland) and other government-funded cleaning support.

Our clients get the same small, consistent team every visit. People they can trust in their home, who take the time to understand their needs and treat their home with care and discretion.

How funded cleaning with us works

1. Get in touch

Call us or send a quote request. You, a family member or your support coordinator can make contact.

2. Assessment

We talk through your needs, your home and anything that matters for your health or comfort.

3. A plan that fits

We agree a cleaning schedule and scope that suits you and your funding arrangement.

4. The same trusted team

Consistent cleaners who know your home, with every visit checked for quality.

NIISQ & Funded Cleaning questions

Are you an approved NIISQ provider?

Yes. We're approved to provide cleaning services for NIISQ participants, as well as other externally funded programs.

Who can arrange the service?

Participants, family members and support coordinators can all contact us. We're happy to work with whoever helps manage your supports.

Will I have the same cleaner each time?

Yes. Consistency matters, especially in someone's home during recovery. We keep a small team so you see familiar faces.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Birkdale and cover Brisbane Bayside including Wellington Point, Cleveland, Thornlands, Redland Bay and Victoria Point.
