Recurring Domestic Clean questions

How often should I book a regular clean?

Fortnightly suits most households. Busy families, pet owners and anyone with health needs often prefer weekly. Monthly works well for smaller homes that stay tidy day to day.

Will I get the same cleaner each time?

Yes. We're a small boutique team and we keep it that way so you get consistent people who know your home and your preferences.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. There are no lock-in contracts. You can change your frequency, pause or stop at any time.

What's the difference between Basic and Premium?

Basic covers the essentials every visit. Premium adds enhanced attention to detail, so more of the edges, fittings and finishes are covered on rotation. We'll recommend the right fit when we quote.

Do you clean in my suburb?

We're based in Birkdale and clean regularly across Brisbane Bayside, including Wellington Point, Cleveland, Thornlands, Redland Bay and Victoria Point.
